## Daily training rules

### Warm-up

- Keep warm-ups to 5–10 minutes of mobility and dynamic movements.
- No treadmill or cycling as part of the warm-up.
- Do 1–3 progressively heavier warm-up sets before your first heavy exercise. These don't count as working sets.

### Rest between sets

| Exercise type               | Rest       |
| --------------------------- | ---------- |
| Heavy compound exercises    | 2–3 min    |
| Moderate compound exercises | 90–120 sec |
| Isolation exercises         | 60–90 sec  |
| Core exercises              | 30–60 sec  |
| Calves and light exercises  | 45–75 sec  |

During rest, recover, sip water, prepare your next weight and record your reps. Don't add extra exercises between sets.

### Post-workout routine

1. Walk slowly for 3–5 minutes to cool down.
2. Stretch the muscles trained that day.
3. Hold each stretch for 20–30 seconds.
4. Avoid bouncing or forcing a painful stretch.
5. Hydrate and have a protein-rich meal with carbohydrates to support recovery.

## How to progress

- Choose weights that let you complete the target reps with controlled technique.
- When you can reach the top of the rep range on every set with good form, increase the weight slightly.
- Don't sacrifice form to lift heavier.
- Aim for consistent sleep and recovery.
- Fat loss comes from an overall sustainable calorie deficit; training helps preserve and build muscle. You cannot selectively burn chest or belly fat through exercises for those areas.
